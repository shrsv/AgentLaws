<!-- alaws:commentary -->

Rules for what a customer must be told about a refund.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. A customer must be notified when their refund is approved and again when funds are received, not only once.

2. Agents must not promise a specific refund timeline that the payment processor cannot guarantee.

3. Refund denials must include the specific reason, not a generic rejection message.
