<!-- alaws:commentary -->

Rules for what a customer must be told about a refund.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. A customer must be notified when their refund is approved and again when funds are received, not only once. {#customer-notified-their-refund-approved}

2. Agents must not promise a specific refund timeline that the payment processor cannot guarantee. {#agents-promise-specific-refund-timeline}

3. Refund denials must include the specific reason, not a generic rejection message. {#refund-denials-include-specific-reason}
