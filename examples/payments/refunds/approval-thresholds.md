<!-- alaws:commentary -->

Rules for how much of a refund an agent can approve on its own authority.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Agent {{agent_name}} may approve a refund up to {{amount}} {{currency}} without additional sign-off.

2. A refund above the agent's approval threshold must be routed to a human approver with the original transaction attached.

3. Refunds must not be split into smaller amounts to stay under an approval threshold.
