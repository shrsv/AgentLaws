<!-- alaws:commentary -->

Rules for status updates and customer-facing messaging during and after an
incident.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Incident {{incident_id}} at severity {{severity}} must have a status update posted at least once per hour until resolved.

2. Customer-facing communication about an incident must be reviewed by a human before it is sent.

3. Post-incident reviews must be published within five business days of resolution.
