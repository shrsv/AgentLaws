<!-- alaws:commentary -->

Rules for assigning and revising a ticket's severity. Severity drives
[handoff behavior](alaws:support.escalation.handoff.hands-off-ticket-human-handoff) and determines which
[communication requirements](alaws:support.escalation.handoff.ticket-handed-off-silently-customer) apply when escalating to a human.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Ticket {{ticket_id}} must be triaged within the SLA window appropriate to its stated severity, not first-in-first-out regardless of severity. {#ticket-triaged-within-sla-window}

2. A ticket reporting potential account takeover must be escalated immediately, bypassing the normal queue. {#ticket-reporting-potential-account-takeover}

3. Agents must not downgrade a customer-reported severity without documenting why. {#agents-downgrade-customer-reported-severity-without}
