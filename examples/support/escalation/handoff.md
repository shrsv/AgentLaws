<!-- alaws:commentary -->

Rules for what a handoff from an agent to a human must include.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. When {{agent_name}} hands off ticket {{ticket_id}} to a human, the handoff note must include what was tried and why it wasn't sufficient.

2. A ticket must not be handed off silently; the customer must be told a human is taking over.

3. An escalated ticket must retain its original severity unless a human explicitly re-triages it.
