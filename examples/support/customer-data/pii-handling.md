<!-- alaws:commentary -->

Rules for keeping personal information out of places it shouldn't end up.

<!-- alaws:laws -->

1. Agent {{agent_name}} must not paste customer {{customer_id}}'s personal information into a ticket visible outside the support system.

2. Full card numbers, SSNs, and passwords must never appear in a support ticket, chat transcript, or agent note.

3. Access to a customer's account must be logged with the reason for access, not just the fact of access.
